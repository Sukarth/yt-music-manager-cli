# Privacy Policy for YT Music Manager CLI

**Effective Date:** October 8, 2025

## Overview

YT Music Manager CLI ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our YouTube Music playlist synchronization tool.

## Information We Collect

### Authentication Data
- **Google OAuth Tokens**: When you authenticate with Google for accessing private playlists, we store encrypted OAuth tokens locally on your device
- **No Data Transmission**: Authentication tokens are never transmitted to our servers - all processing happens locally on your device

### Local Data Storage
- **Playlist Information**: YouTube playlist URLs and metadata are stored locally
- **Download History**: Information about downloaded files is kept locally for sync purposes
- **Configuration Settings**: Your app preferences and settings are stored locally

## How We Use Your Information

### Core Functionality
- **Playlist Synchronization**: To download and sync your YouTube playlists to local MP3 files
- **Authentication**: To access your YouTube account for private/unlisted playlists
- **Local Processing**: All data processing occurs on your local device

### No External Data Sharing
- We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers
- No analytics, tracking, or usage data is collected
- No user data is shared with third parties

## Data Security

### Local Storage Only
- All data remains on your local device
- OAuth tokens are encrypted using device-specific keys
- No cloud storage or remote backups

### Third-Party Services
- **Google YouTube API**: Used only for playlist access with your explicit consent
- **YouTube Downloads**: Handled through yt-dlp library with no data collection

## Your Rights

### Data Control
- You can delete all local data at any time
- Authentication can be revoked through Google account settings
- App can be uninstalled to remove all stored data

### Google Account Access
- You can revoke app access anytime at: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
- The app requests read-only access to your YouTube data
